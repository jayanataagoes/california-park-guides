Zuma and Point Dume: Planning a Malibu County Beach Day

Malibu’s two best-known county beaches sit only about a mile and a half apart, but they work for different kinds of days. Here is how to choose, and what to check before you drive Pacific Coast Highway.

Zuma County Beach: space and amenities

Zuma is the big, easy one. The county describes 1.8 miles of beach frontage, eight parking lots, and roughly 2,000 parking spaces, so finding room for a towel is rarely the problem. Parking is in a paid county lot, and lifeguards cover the beach during daylight hours. Food stands sit at each end of the sand, and the facilities include restrooms, showers, and volleyball nets. Beach wheelchairs are available, and there is a bus stop on Pacific Coast Highway at Busch. The address is 30000 Pacific Coast Highway.

One current caution: the park guide notes that the Busch Drive and PCH underpass is closed because of flooding, while the beach, lots, and restrooms remain open. Cross the highway the way the signs direct you, and check the official notice before you go. For the full fact sheet, see this Zuma County Beach guide.

Point Dume County Beach: scenery and a short climb

Point Dume is smaller and more scenic. The county lists more than a mile of ocean frontage framed by headlands, cliffs, and rocky coves. A hiking trail climbs to a coastal bluff with views across Santa Monica Bay, and on clear days you can see Catalina Island. From December to March the bluff is one of the better places in the area to watch gray whales migrate. A stairway on the east side of the bluff top leads down to a quieter beach with good tide pooling, so bring shoes with grip and check the tide table first.

Parking is a paid lot with 373 spaces on Westward Beach Road. Lifeguards are on duty in daylight hours, and the beach has restrooms and showers. Metro bus 534 stops at Cliffside Drive and Dume Drive for anyone who would rather skip the lot.

There is an important access note. The park guide reports that erosion has closed the beach access road beyond the pay gate until further notice, so confirm current access on the county page before you plan a Point Dume day. Details are in the Point Dume County Beach page.

Dogs

Both beaches follow the LA County beach rules, and dogs are not allowed on the beach at either one. If your dog is part of the family outing, pick an inland park instead.

Which one to choose

  • Choose Zuma for a big group, kids, volleyball, and food stands within walking distance.
  • Choose Point Dume for views, whale watching in winter, and tide pooling.
  • Go early on weekends in summer. Lots fill, and lifeguard coverage is only during daylight.

Quick checklist

  • Check the official county notice for closures before you leave.
  • Bring layers, since Malibu mornings can be cool and breezy.
  • Pack water, sunscreen, and shoes you can scramble in.
  • Plan to leave the dog at home.

Beach conditions, parking rates, and closures change, so treat this as a planning guide and confirm the details with LA County Beaches and Harbors on the day.
